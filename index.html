How do councils get Australians taking part?

A year of council events across Australia

Explore

How often do Australians take part, and where? Library programs are one of the few council activities with public attendance figures, so they let us compare whole states, single councils and a full year of events in Brisbane.

Compare

Councils run plenty of events. The real differences are in who they are for, which sessions draw a crowd, and when they run.

About the data

State totals are for 2023–24, council figures for 2024–25 and events for 2025.

Council figures cover New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland. Other states do not publish them council by council.

Brisbane's events have no attendance. Townsville's attendance covers library programs only.

Brisbane's full year of events was read from the council's calendar feed on 10 October 2026.
