How do councils get Australians to take part?

A year of local council events across Australia

Explore

Library programs are one of the few council activities with public attendance figures, so they show how often people take part, from whole states down to single councils. A year of events in Brisbane then shows how much a city council puts on.

Compare

Not every program draws people in equally. Comparing audiences, councils, session sizes and seasons shows who councils reach most, which programs bring the biggest crowds, and when people turn up.

Investigate

Councils can make taking part easier by removing cost and booking, using local venues, and going out to where people already are. The last chart asks whether residents feel their council listens to them.
